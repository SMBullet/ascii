Mitigations

Use per-host (SAN) certificates where practical. Issue individual certificates (or scoped SAN certs) per service so a compromise is contained to that host rather than the full subdomain space.
Automate issuance and rotation. Adopt short-lived certificates with automated management (e.g. ACME/Let’s Encrypt or an internal CA) so per-host certs are operationally cheap and keys rotate frequently.
Protect and segregate private keys. Restrict wildcard-key distribution, store keys in an HSM or secrets manager, avoid deploying the same key to low-trust or third-party systems, and have a tested revocation/re-issuance plan.
